External Library / Development Log
====================================

[2026-09-02 / 2026-09-03]

Game specifications recorded:
1. Game direction: GTA風の探索・アクション・アドベンチャー型3Dゲーム。
2. Third-person rear-view camera.
3. Player is a human-like character with a natural body structure.
4. Movement uses WASD or Arrow Keys; Arrow Keys may also be assigned to camera direction when useful.
5. Stage is wilderness / wasteland with terrain undulation and natural objects.
6. Visual/display information and internal/game/physics information must be handled separately and kept consistent; physics/internal state is authoritative for physical placement.
7. Sun altitude, direction, illumination, shadows and environmental light should be represented faithfully and naturally according to in-game time.
8. NPCs are part of the game world.
9. NPCs are action/behavior-only rather than conversation-driven.
10. NPCs may be somewhat lighter than the player, but must not be arbitrarily simplified during development.
11. Z + W is the run action; input must support at least three simultaneous keys.
12. Player movement is being built with acceleration/deceleration, grounded movement, jumping, terrain contact, camera collision handling and human-like movement animation.

External libraries:
- Three.js 0.180.0: browser-side 3D rendering, lighting, camera and human model generation.
  Source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.180.0/build/three.module.js
- Rapier 3D 0.19.0: browser-side physics simulation, gravity and collision handling.
  Source: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@dimforge/rapier3d-compat@0.19.0/+esm

Implementation notes:
- physics.js owns the Rapier world/body/collider helpers.
- game.js owns Three.js, input, game loop, camera, NPC behavior and visual synchronization.
- Player and NPC positions are read from Rapier after each physics step before updating Three.js models.
- Player movement uses impulses derived from velocity error rather than directly teleporting the physics body.
- Player grounding ray excludes the player's own collider to avoid false grounded states.
